Ask any Dubai salon owner what clients request most this year and a surprising answer keeps coming up: less. Less makeup, less morning effort, less time in front of the mirror. Brow lamination and lash lifts — two treatments that make brows look fuller and lashes look curled for weeks — have quietly become one of the most requested additions to UAE salon menus.

Neither treatment is new. What changed is the client: UAE women want results that survive a gym session, a beach afternoon and a 45-degree summer day. A treatment that holds up in Dubai humidity and still looks good at 7pm beats a dramatic look that melts by lunchtime.

## What the treatments actually do

Brow lamination relaxes brow hairs and sets them in a brushed-up, fuller direction. It does not add hair. It reorganises the hair a client already has, which is why it works so well on the sparse, patchy brows that over-plucking left behind. The result lasts four to six weeks, and the appointment usually takes 45 minutes including a tint.

A lash lift does the same idea for eyelashes. Lashes are lifted from the root onto a silicone shield and set into a curl, usually tinted so the curl shows without mascara. Results last six to eight weeks, roughly the natural lash cycle, and the appointment runs about an hour.

There is no downtime with either treatment, no healing period and no aftercare routine longer than keeping the area dry for 24 hours. That simplicity is the whole appeal — and it is also why salons that explain them clearly tend to sell them easily.

## Why the UAE market fits them so well

The UAE is almost a perfect market for low-maintenance beauty. First, the climate. Heat and humidity punish heavy makeup, and clients who swim, train or spend weekends at the beach want a look that does not wash off. A lash lift survives the pool in a way mascara never will.

Second, the lifestyle. Many UAE clients juggle long working hours with school runs, gym sessions and social evenings. A treatment that saves ten minutes every morning for six weeks is an easy sell when the value is explained in time rather than dirhams.

## The numbers for a salon owner

This is where the trend becomes a business decision rather than a fashion one. Both treatments use inexpensive consumables — lifting solutions, shields, tint and disposables typically cost a salon 15 to 30 dirhams per client. Training takes one or two days, and the service needs only a treatment bed, good lighting and a steady hand.

Pricing in Dubai typically sits between 150 and 350 dirhams for a lash lift and 150 to 300 dirhams for brow lamination, with many salons offering the two together as a package around 400 to 500 dirhams. At those prices, a stylist doing four combined appointments a day adds meaningful revenue without adding a chair, a room or a new hire.

## Who it is for — and who should skip it

Honest screening is what separates salons that keep these clients from salons that get complaints. Brow lamination suits clients with unruly, downward-growing or sparse brows. It is a poor choice for very thin brows, and it should never be sold as a replacement for microblading.

Lash lifts suit clients with straight or downward-pointing lashes who want a natural curl. They are not for everyone. Very short lashes will see only a subtle result, and anyone with an eye infection, recent eye surgery or a known sensitivity should wait.

## Fitting it into the diary

The practical question is where these services live in a busy diary. The good news is that they fit the quiet hours beautifully. A lash lift takes an hour of a therapist's time but needs no stylist and no chair, so it can run in a treatment room while the cutting floor is fully booked. Many salons place them in late-morning and early-afternoon slots and keep peak evening hours for cuts and colour.

## Marketing it without a filter

The temptation with any beauty trend is to post heavily filtered before-and-after photos. Resist it. Clients in the UAE have seen enough edited images to distrust them, and a lash lift that looks dramatic in a photo and subtle in the mirror is a complaint waiting to happen.

Honest close-up photos in natural light — same eye, same angle, before and after — work better, and a short video of the process does more than any caption. And the most effective marketing of all is the client herself. A lash lift is visible every time she opens her eyes, and her friends will ask where she had it done. Make it easy for her to answer by putting the service name, price range and booking link in the salon's WhatsApp catalogue, so the recommendation turns into a booking in the same conversation.

That is also when enquiries arrive at odd hours. A client sees a friend's lashes at a 10pm dinner and messages the salon straight away. ## The aftercare conversation matters

The difference between a client who rebooks and a client who never returns is often the last two minutes of the appointment. Both treatments need 24 hours without water, steam or heavy products. Clients who hear that clearly, and get it again in a short WhatsApp message that evening, follow it. Clients who are rushed out the door do not — and then blame the treatment.

A simple aftercare message, sent the same evening, costs nothing and does two jobs at once. It protects the result, and it makes the rebooking message six weeks later feel personal rather than promotional.

## A trend with staying power

Not every beauty trend survives its first year. Brow lamination and lash lifts look like they will, because they are not really about fashion. They are about time. They give busy clients back a few minutes every morning, and they give salons a service that is cheap to offer, easy to schedule and naturally repeatable.

For UAE salon owners, the question is not whether the demand is real, but whether the salon is ready — trained hands, honest consultations, clear pricing and a booking process that catches the client the moment she decides. Get those right, and a pair of small treatments can quietly become one of the steadiest earners in the diary.
