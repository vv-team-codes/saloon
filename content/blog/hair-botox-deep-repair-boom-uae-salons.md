Walk into any Dubai salon on a Thursday evening and the story is the same: hair worn down by months of sun, sea, swimming pools, daily straighteners and hard water. It looks dry at the ends, feels rough to the touch, and no home conditioner brings back the shine. Clients say it the same way: "My hair feels like straw. Can you fix it?"

That question is driving one of the busiest new services on UAE salon menus. Hair botox — a deep-repair treatment that fills in damaged hair from the inside — is being booked out across Dubai, and it is becoming a reliable second service for clients who already come in for cuts and colour.

## First, what hair botox actually is

The name causes confusion, so clear it up first. Hair botox has nothing to do with injectable Botox or needles. It is a conditioning treatment — a mask or serum rich in proteins, amino acids, vitamins and oils — worked through the hair and sealed in with heat. The name is shorthand: the treatment fills the gaps in a damaged hair shaft, so hair feels fuller, smoother and shinier.

It is not a straightening treatment. Unlike keratin, it does not change the hair's structure or straighten curls — it repairs and smooths. That matters here, where many clients want their natural wave kept, just healthier. Results last six to eight weeks, making it a repeat service rather than a one-off fix — which is why salon owners like it.

## Why the UAE climate makes it a natural fit

Dubai's conditions are unusually hard on hair. Intense sun breaks down the hair's outer layer. Humidity swells the shaft and lifts the cuticle, which is why frizz is the most common complaint in UAE salons. Hard water leaves deposits that dull shine and fade colour faster. Add daily heat styling and the damage compounds week after week.

The treatment sells itself once clients understand it. A client who colours her hair every six weeks, swims twice a week and straightens daily is the perfect candidate, and there are thousands of her in every neighbourhood. The salon's job is to explain it clearly and price it honestly.

## Who it suits — and who it does not

Honesty is what turns a trend into a reputation. Hair botox suits dry, damaged, frizzy or colour-treated hair best, and fine hair that has gone limp, because it adds body without weight. It does not suit everyone: very healthy, virgin hair will see little difference, and a good stylist says so rather than selling a treatment the client does not need. Extremely damaged hair — hair that snaps when pulled — needs cutting and a longer repair plan, not a single treatment.

Spend five minutes at the mirror first: feel the hair, ask about swimming, straightening and colour history. That prevents the disappointed client who expected a miracle. Promise honestly — smoother, shinier, easier to manage, for six to eight weeks — and then over-deliver on the experience.

## Pricing it and fitting it into the diary

A treatment typically takes 60 to 90 minutes, depending on hair length and thickness — a natural add-on to a cut or colour appointment, or a standalone booking in a quiet weekday slot. Many UAE salons price it by hair length — short, medium, long — which clients understand instantly and stops the awkward "how much will it cost for me?" message before it is sent.

Price it as a premium conditioning service, not a luxury treatment — in most Dubai salons it sits between a gloss and a keratin treatment. Too cheap and clients doubt it works; too expensive and they try it once and never return. The sweet spot is a price that makes the six-to-eight-week repeat feel easy. It pairs beautifully with colour — colour first, hair botox after — and one trained stylist can run two treatments in parallel during processing time, which makes the service quietly profitable.

## Aftercare is where the repeat booking is won

The result depends on what happens after the client leaves, so aftercare is the retention plan. Send a short WhatsApp note: sulphate-free shampoo, no washing for 24 to 48 hours, a heat protectant before styling. Keep it to four lines.

Then book the follow-up before she leaves. A simple "Shall I put you in for the same time in seven weeks?" at the mirror converts a one-off treatment into a routine. The rebooking rate is one of the highest of any salon service, because the result fades gradually and the client notices the moment it does. Many first-time clients are back within two months — and while they are in the chair, they book their colour and cut too.

## Marketing it without overpromising

The videos that convert are the honest ones: real before-and-after shots of the same client, with the price range and time written on the screen. UAE clients are quick to spot filtered, over-lit transformations, and honest results build the trust that turns a viewer into a booking. Do not promise what the treatment cannot do — it will not straighten curly hair, repair split ends without a trim, or last forever. Saying that plainly is the reason clients believe everything else you say.

Enquiries arrive late at night, as they always do — a client watching a video at 11pm wants the price for her hair length and a Friday slot. Answering quickly, with the price range and two real times, often wins the booking over the salon that replies the next morning. ## A simple way to start

Start small. Train one stylist properly, on real clients, first. Offer it first to your existing colour clients — they already trust you, and their hair is the best advertisement. Run it for a month, track the rebooking rate, then put it on Instagram and WhatsApp status. A trend service added carefully becomes a permanent part of the menu; one added in a rush becomes a complaint.

The UAE climate is not going to get kinder to hair. Sun, humidity, hard water and daily heat styling are part of life here, and clients will keep looking for a treatment that genuinely helps. Hair botox, done honestly and priced fairly, answers a real, everyday problem. That is why salons are booking it out.
