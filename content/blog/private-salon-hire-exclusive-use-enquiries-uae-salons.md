## The enquiry that sounds like a dream booking

"Can we book the whole salon for my birthday?" It arrives on WhatsApp, usually in the evening, and it sounds like the easiest money a salon will ever make. A group of friends, a celebration, the whole place to themselves — and one person ready to pay for it.

It can also be the booking that costs a salon a Saturday evening and four regulars. The difference is how the salon answers the enquiry. Private hire is a growing request in the UAE — birthdays, bridal showers, graduation parties — and the salons that handle it well turn it into some of their best-paying days.

## What she is really asking

Nobody asks to hire a whole salon for the chairs. She is asking five quieter questions, and the salon that answers all five in the first reply wins the booking. Will it actually be private — just her group, no strangers walking in? How much will it cost: one flat fee, or a price per chair? How long do they get the place? Can they bring cake, balloons, music and a photographer? And what happens to her deposit if two friends cancel at the last minute?

She is not asking for a discount. Groups that want the whole salon expect to pay for the privilege. Salons lose these bookings by being vague or slow, not by being expensive.

## The four-part reply that books it

The best replies follow the same shape every time, and they fit in one message.

First, warmth and a clear answer on the date. "We would love to host your group — Saturday 14 December from 4 to 7 PM is available for exclusive use." That tells her this is a real offer.

Second, name the package, not a price list. "Our private hire package includes the whole salon for up to 10 guests, a blow-dry or express facial for each guest, welcome drinks and a dedicated host." A package is easier to say yes to than per-person prices.

Third, state the price and the deposit together. "The package is AED 2,500 for the three hours, with a 50% deposit to confirm."

Fourth, end with one concrete next step and a deadline. "Shall I hold Saturday 4 to 7 PM for you until tomorrow evening while you confirm numbers?" That protects the diary while she confirms.

## Pricing private hire honestly

Two mistakes are common: charging what ten individual appointments would cost, and charging a flat fee so low the salon would have earned more from a normal Saturday.

The honest way to price it is to start with what the salon would normally earn in those hours, then add the value of exclusivity. If a Saturday evening normally brings in AED 3,000 across the chairs, the private hire price starts there — not below it. Exclusivity, a host, drinks and the freedom to play their own music are worth a premium on top, because the group is buying something a normal client cannot buy: the whole room.

Colour, extensions and bridal trials do not belong in a three-hour party package unless priced separately — say so in the first message, kindly: "Colour and extensions need their own appointments — we can book those for the week before, so everyone looks their best on the day."

## Diary fit: when to say yes

Not every slot suits private hire, and a salon should know its answer before the enquiry arrives. The best hours are the ones that are hard to fill anyway — a Sunday morning, a weekday late afternoon, the quiet hours before the evening rush. A Saturday from 5 to 9 PM is the worst time to hand a salon to one group, because that is when regulars book, and regulars are worth more than one party.

Offer private hire outside peak hours, or at a price that genuinely beats a peak evening — the price should reflect what the salon gives up.

Decide the maximum group size in advance, too. A salon with six chairs cannot comfortably host fourteen guests, no matter how much they are paying. Ten guests works; fourteen in the same room is a queue, not a party.

## The deposit and the small print

Private hire without a deposit is a favour, not a booking. Half up front is the standard that works in the UAE, paid by card or payment link, with the balance due on the day. Write the rules in one short message before she pays: the deposit holds the date, it is non-refundable inside seven days, and guest numbers can change up to 48 hours before.

Groups always shrink. If the salon has promised a per-person price, every cancellation becomes a negotiation. A flat package price with a flexible guest count — "up to 10 guests" — removes the argument entirely. The price stays the same; the group brings who they can.

Most other private hire problems are not about money. They are about cake on the chairs, a speaker at full volume, or a photographer blocking the walkway. Answer these before the day, in writing, with warmth: "You are welcome to bring a cake and balloons — we will set up a small table for them. Music is yours to choose, and photos are welcome in the corner we keep clear for them."

## The follow-up that turns one party into regulars

The booking does not end when the party leaves. Every guest is a potential regular who has just spent three hours inside the salon.

A short WhatsApp message the next day, to the organiser and each guest, turns a one-off event into a pipeline. "Thank you for celebrating with us yesterday. If any of your friends would like to come back, their next visit is on us for a blow-dry."
