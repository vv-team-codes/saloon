## Why Meta Ads Deserve a Place in a Salon's Marketing

Most salon owners in the UAE already post on Instagram and Facebook. Organic posts keep regulars warm, but they reach only a fraction of the people nearby who have never heard of your salon. That is the gap Meta ads fill. With a modest budget, you can put a single service in front of women living or working within a few kilometres of your chair, at the exact moment they are scrolling for inspiration.

## Start With One Service, Not Your Whole Menu

The most common mistake is advertising everything at once. A carousel of twenty services tells a stranger nothing. Pick the service that is easiest to understand in three seconds, photographs well, and has a clear price range. A balayage, a keratin treatment, a bridal trial or a signature facial all work. A vague "we do hair and beauty" does not.

## Target the Streets Around You, Not the Whole UAE

A salon is a local business, and Meta's targeting is at its best when you keep it local. Set your radius to three to five kilometres around your location, or target the specific communities your clients actually come from — the residential towers near a mall salon, the villas around a high-street branch, the offices near a business-district location.

## Use Real Work, Not Stock Photos

People scroll past polished stock images without thinking. They stop for a real result on a real client. Use a before-and-after photo or a short video clip of your own work, taken in your salon, in good light, with the client's permission. Show the hair from the back as well as the front, so the viewer can judge the result properly.

Add one line of honest context: the service, how long it took, and the price range. "Balayage, 3 hours, from AED 450" answers the three questions a stranger asks before messaging. An ad that hides the price gets curious clicks and few bookings. An ad that states it gets fewer clicks — and more of the right ones.

## Send Clicks to WhatsApp, Not to a Website Nobody Reads

Salon clients in the UAE book through conversation. When someone taps your ad, the easiest next step is a WhatsApp chat that opens with a ready-made message: "Hi, I'd like to book the balayage offer." From there, your team can confirm a time in two or three messages.

## Set a Budget You Can Sustain for Three Weeks

A useful starting point is AED 30 to AED 50 a day for a single service campaign. That is roughly AED 1,000 a month — enough data to learn, small enough to stop without pain if it is not working. The mistake is spending AED 500 in two days, panicking at the result, and switching everything off. Meta's system needs a few days to find the people who respond, and you need a few days to see which messages turn into bookings.

## Test One Thing at a Time

Change the photo, keep the text. Change the headline, keep the photo. Change the audience, keep everything else. If you change all three at once, you will never know what worked. Keep testing simple: two versions of the same ad, running side by side for a week, with the same budget.

## When to Run Ads, and When to Pause Them

Timing matters as much as targeting. The best moments to advertise are the days before your quiet hours, not after them. If Tuesdays and Wednesdays are slow, run the ad from Sunday to Tuesday so the bookings land where the gaps are. If your evenings are already full, do not pay to fill them further — point the ad at the morning or afternoon slots that need help.

## The Mistakes That Waste the Budget

Three errors eat most salon ad budgets in the UAE. The first is targeting too widely — the whole country, all ages, no interests. The second is sending clicks to a dead end: a phone number nobody answers after 7 pm, a WhatsApp number that replies the next morning. The third is running an ad with no offer at all, just a logo and a slogan. A stranger needs a reason to act this week: a named service, a price range, and a time slot that is actually free.

## What Good Looks Like After a Month

After three or four weeks, you should be able to answer four questions. Which service booked best from ads? Which photo or video stopped the scroll? Which neighbourhood produced the most clients? And what did each booking cost? Write the answers down. They become your playbook for the next campaign, and they stop you repeating the same spend on the same guess.

Start small, start local, and start with one service you are proud of. The chairs will tell you whether it worked.
